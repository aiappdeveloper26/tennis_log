Match tapping

For a match or practice match. Every result is on this one panel, so you tap after each ball without switching skills. Serve rules: if a first serve misses, tap Net, Long, or Wide on First serve, then tap the second serve result. For an ace, tap Ace instead of In. A double fault is one tap on Dbl fault, which also counts a missed second serve. Undo removes one tap at a time, so press it as many times as you need.

Counts are saved on this device as you go.
